WalkBPM+

  • To cancel or change your plan, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Got a new iPhone? Open WalkBPM+ in the app's Settings and tap Restore Purchases.
  • Have an offer code? Tap Redeem code on the WalkBPM+ page.

Apple Health

If walks aren't appearing as workouts, open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps (under Privacy), then WalkBPM, and turn on every category.

Your data
